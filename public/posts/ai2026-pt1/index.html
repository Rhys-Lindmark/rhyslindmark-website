READING THE CHART

One chart. Two measures.

Solid lines show net profit, adjusted to 2026 dollars. The left axis is logarithmic: equal distances represent equal multiples. Dashed lines show estimated average operating margins on the right axis—not net-profit margins.

Wintel combines Microsoft and Intel. GAFA combines Google/Alphabet, Apple, Meta and Amazon. Nvidia begins in FY2022. These are different-sized groups over different periods, rather than like-for-like company comparisons.

Prototype based on the original “Profit & Margins Across Tech Eras” chart from August 28, 2026. Profit values and rounded, period-weighted margin estimates are preserved from that chart; they have not been freshly audited. CPI conversion uses the original dataset’s 2026 reference value.
