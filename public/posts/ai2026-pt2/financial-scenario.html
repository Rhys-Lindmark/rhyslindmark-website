AI lab financial scenarios

Working estimates as of October 9, 2026. The slides combine recent annualized revenue paces with explicit annual expense assumptions. They are scenarios, not reported current income statements. Cash payroll is estimated from headcount; stock compensation is excluded throughout. Revenue accounting also differs: Anthropic reports partner sales gross, while OpenAI treats its arrangements differently. Revenue accounting comparison.

Training and research compute includes pre-training, RL, experiments, and research infrastructure. Researchers’ cash pay belongs in salaries; it is not counted again in compute. “Other” excludes payroll, compute, and stock compensation.

The Q1 anchor is reported R&D of $8.6B, operating loss of $9.3B, and stock compensation above $2.3B. Q1 shareholder-document reporting.

Our reconstruction estimates total Q1 opex at $11.5B. For compute, subtract assumed research stock compensation ($1.8B), research cash pay ($0.4B), and noncompute research costs ($0.6B) from R&D: $8.6B − $1.8B − $0.4B − $0.6B = $5.8B. Company-wide cash payroll is estimated at $0.65B per quarter. Remaining opex is $11.5B − $5.8B − $0.65B − $2.3B = $2.75B. These splits are our assumptions, not company disclosures.

The earlier $52.3B compute and $28.2B “salaries & opex” figures multiplied Q1 expense shares by a later revenue pace. This scenario stops that extrapolation. Stock compensation could add materially to costs; neither model estimates it for the current period.
