AI 2026 · Part 2, Models

OpenAI and Anthropic are massive.

They’re the fastest-growing companies we’ve ever seen.

There is a massive difference vs. Nvidia though — Ant & OAI have terrible margins.

OpenAI and Ant both spend $60 on COGS (inference) to make $100 of revenue.

But OpenAI spends ~$160 on opex (training R&D) vs. Anthropic’s $40.

This chart breaks down Anthropic’s business.

They make $30B/GW. It costs them $9B to pay hyperscalers to serve that inference. With the remaining $21B, they pour $8B into compute for the next training cluster and $12B on people.

OpenAI doesn’t seem to have the same margins yet.

We’ll see if OpenAI can run the margin expansion playbook that Amazon, Uber, and Anthropic have run. They have a lot of ground to cover:

Compute is a massive bill for both OAI and Ant. As an example, OAI spent $7B on compute in 2024.

They want commoditized compute to help with their margins.

Both for training,

inference,

and for solving Millennium Prize problems.

Yes, this result cost millions of dollars.

But remember that when @OpenAI announced o3 it cost ~$500,000 to score 87.5% on ARC-AGI 1. Today, Astra scores higher for ~$20.
