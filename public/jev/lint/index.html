The five checks, before you send a question

  1. The human test. Could a smart person, shown only this input, answer at a glance?
  2. Judgment, not decision. “Is production down?”, not “Should we escalate?”
  3. Beware the “or”. “Rude or drunk?” is two Nouls. Combine them in code.
  4. Ask about nothing. A Choice always picks. Pair it with a Noul: “does anything fit?”
  5. Math goes in code. Read the number or date; compare it in code. Jev isn’t a calculator.
